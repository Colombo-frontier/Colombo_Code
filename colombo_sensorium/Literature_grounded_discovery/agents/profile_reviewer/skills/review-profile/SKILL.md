# Independent Profile and Bound Update Reviewer Skill

## Review steps

For open-question profiles, verify that the exact joint target remains a requested research goal outside active proved bounds. Check that Missing bounds, unresolved resource accounting, and any source-contract-family scope are explicit and agree with approved identity. No free geometry, assumed machine model, selector refinement, randomized guarantee, or present-day openness claim may enter through prose or empty bound lists. Apply `standards/identity.md` to any remaining primary-interface uncertainty; document approval does not approve the missing model or target.

Read proof_context_ref to distinguish a reusable independent proposition from its current application. First-time proof credentials require full verification and complete context. Unchanged certified proofs may use catalog-verified reuse; stable proofs with context changes may instead use the independent scope revalidation below. Neither path waives new counterexamples, source defects, complete issue scopes or current application/envelope/five-file review. Do not omit assumptions to increase reuse rates.

1. First call schema_read(review). Read research_ref, candidate/identity, profile_ref, prepared_ref, and mode from the frozen proposal; prepared is the final five-file snapshot. For action=no_change, the subject is research directly; verify its no-change justification as well. Load `standards/evidence.md`, `standards/bracket_reasoning.md`, and `standards/profile.md`; read the corresponding standards for new specifications/labels. Do not reread the entire annual bibliography queue.
2. Verify agreement between specification and approved identity. Read differences and credential candidates first. Reuse requires verified role/subject/snapshot/context and complete mathematical dependencies. Recheck proofs in full without credentials, after mathematical dependency changes or when assumptions are undeclared. Catalog-listed scope revalidation instead examines changed context and affected applications independently; it never silently inherits approval. Explicitly list every active bound and approve the current proposal. A first new-profile review is complete, not summary-only.
3. For every bound proposed for activation, verify the six comparison keys, actual upper witness/lower algorithm class, guarantee/cost quantifiers, full parameter domain, and source version. Check decisive steps in each analyst proof; a similar formula in a source paper is not a proof.
4. Pay particular attention to simulation costs/additional oracles, metric/probability conversions, fixed-object and worst-case quantifiers, randomized adaptive lower bounds, dimension lattices/padding, and coarse accuracy. An erroneous source lemma requires inspection of its dependencies and repairs; it does not automatically refute an independent main theorem.
5. Recheck envelopes and complete all nine coverage_audit checks. Consolidate the finite delivery's known executable obligations in the first review, including all already identified family parameters and endpoints. Later reviews start from delivery_scope_ref, previous resolutions and actual changes. A new counterexample, unsupported adopted bound, false optimality or source/applicability defect may always block; identify the affected claim and exact missing proof step. Do not turn verification into repeated optimization of another valid algorithm's constants. A newly derived compatible stronger construction beyond the fixed frontier uses a typed evidence-bearing followup and the native joint-parameter pending obligation when existing claims remain valid. Retain the actual stronger result, proof and affected cells; do not call it unassessed when already verified. If the current gap/status itself is false, repair that claim first. Registration changes need the author's formal resolution and a new frozen review; an explicitly partial registered envelope does not claim global sharpness.
6. Verify self-contained English, complete references/proofs, and, when reader_markdown exists, its point-by-point agreement with structured scientific fields. Verify agreement of the final prepared five files' eight-axis labels/parents, Exact scope, identity/aliases, and body; inspect statuses, gap_causes, and formula/table presentation. Use vocabulary_lookup for concept definitions and registered parent edges rather than guessing classes from keywords. Do not claim visual acceptance if rendering was not performed.
7. Fill approved_bound_ids per bound and quarantine unproved/inapplicable results. A document that honestly preserves a missing side may be approved. Unknown native identity, invalid versions, or an unsupported active bracket -> revise/defer. Historical issues may be partly resolved; an arbitrary unresolved item must not automatically reject the document.
8. Save the review. Review approval establishes adoption conditions only; the final writer still checks versions, identity, and transactions and issues an intake receipt.

## Detailed handoff rules

### Version focused-scope-review: independent context and scope revalidation

The scope/execution contract remains `issue_scope_version=occurrence-bound-scope`. Start from the proof catalog, using `read_batch` for independent reads. Its `proof_scope_revalidation_candidates` are distinct from automatic reuse candidates. Each identifies a stable formal proof and mathematical dependency closure, an actual previous independent credential, the old/current proposal and profile refs, a controller-generated `candidate_id`, `context_changes`, and `new_issue_occurrence_ids`. The catalog's scientific-material fingerprint and context digests are identity checks, not a judgment that the changed application is valid. Read bounded leaf changes and their full source pointers; fetch omitted or ambiguous context rather than treating a bounded summary as complete evidence.

For a listed scope candidate, inspect the original credential and all changed or previously included context. Independently check whether the fixed proposition still applies and whether a new contextual issue affects it. Revisit earlier issue occurrences as well as later reviewer summaries: an assessment of a later profile does not dispose of a different earlier occurrence. Resolve or explicitly scope each applicable occurrence through `proof_issue_scopes` and `bound_issue_scopes`; preserve direct source/proof/result defects. A copied historical issue is provenance, not a new theorem counterexample, but genuinely new evidence or a changed issue's mathematical content still requires independent examination.

When this job supplies scope-revalidation candidates and an active bound is approved using an affected formal proof, complete the current `proof_issue_scopes` even if you choose full review and leave all optional credentials/revalidation rows empty. Declining a credential cannot erase these newly visible contextual issues; it does not force you to issue a credential either.

After this review, submit `proof_scope_revalidations=[{proof_ref,candidate_id,changes_reviewed:true,reason}]` and a current `proof_approvals` row for the same proof. Set `proof_reuse_context_ref` to this job's actual `proof_context_ref` for binding, even though this path is not reuse. Explain the changed context and why its actual application is valid, or decline the credential. Copy the candidate ID from this input catalog; the packet's compact `review_scope.proof_scope_revalidation_candidates` links directly to each full catalog entry. Do not put a revalidated proof in `reused_proof_refs`: this is a new independent scope judgment, not automatic inheritance. The controller validates the candidate and stable mathematical content; it cannot decide the mathematics for you. Changed proposition/source/proof/dependencies or genuine changes to its assumptions require full review of the affected mathematics instead. Existing execution-applicability checks and all six joint checks still apply.

When issuing a new narrow credential, use `scope=standalone_proposition_explicit_context` only if `context_pointers` explicitly covers `/common_specification` and every additional definition needed for the complete proposition: all mathematical parameters and their ranges, variables/blocks, norms, quantifiers, oracle, guarantee and cost assumptions. Point to individual definitions only when each pointer has a clear complete meaning; include their enclosing field when necessary. This scope avoids implicitly hashing an unrelated entire `/parameters` array. Legacy `standalone_proposition` keeps its conservative whole-parameters behavior and is not retroactively narrowed.

When moving from a legacy credential to explicit context, inspect the old whole context and the excluded definitions; justify their irrelevance in the current reason. A real assumption cannot be dropped by changing the pointer list, and an unknown dependency cannot be certified complete. Scope revalidation permits focused reading of genuinely stable already-certified mathematics, not a waiver of a true counterexample or changed assumptions. Independently approve every current bound application, composition, comparison, reader and five-file snapshot after this check.

### Version occurrence-bound-scope: executed checks and exact issue occurrences

Read `standards/exact_checks.md` for the exact experiment tool syntax, capability limits and receipt interpretation before using it.

Read the packet/proof context's `adopted_proof_requirements` for formal proof refs and bound bindings. For every formal proof supporting a currently adopted bound, declare execution applicability in `proof_execution_checks=[{proof_ref,execution_check}]` or the corresponding `proof_approvals` row. Empty reuse credentials do not waive this requirement. Do not duplicate the declaration; if both are present they must agree. This includes proof dependencies on the adopted proof/result path, but does not turn unrelated provenance into a proof dependency.

For current tasks use `issue_scope_version=occurrence-bound-scope`. Read the `issue_occurrences` catalog in the review packet and both reuse contexts. An issue ID can have different historical occurrences. Fill each scope row's `occurrence_ids` with the complete applicable catalog set, inspecting its original artifact, pointer, variant and subject. A later reviewer statement does not silently resolve an earlier occurrence. Preserve hard blockers carried by the source, result or proof itself. Declare a complete scope only after independent review.

Current-review issue occurrences depend on the actual draft. If preflight reports missing/changed occurrence IDs, read its complete `scope_diagnostic` through `report_ref`. Inspect `current_review_occurrences` and `hard_dependency_blockers`; use `expected_issue_scopes` for the exact required ID sets. Do not calculate these hashes by serializing a draft yourself or invent missing IDs.

Every proof approval includes `execution_check={required, reason, check_refs}`. Decide applicability mathematically. When a proposition depends on an oracle simulation, a decision/branch algorithm, or a source algorithm with exact return semantics, set `required=true`. Use `exact_check` with the authorized proof and original source in `evidence_refs` before issuing its credential. Construct small admissible instances from the definitions; enumerate every structurally distinct branch, including equality/tie, boundary, invalid-domain and degenerate branches where applicable. Record exact rational inputs, each intermediate quantity, the branch decision, the returned value/object and the independently calculated legal return. Check value equality and the defining membership, support, feasibility or oracle error conditions. Use the tool's real execution receipt in `check_refs`, not a manually written assertion of execution. Failed assertions, illegal returns, or branches not verified require investigation and may block that proof and dependent bounds. Do not certify a cited algorithm merely because its theorem or argument was read. A passing finite example is only an auxiliary check; independently verify the full quantified proof, assumptions and dependencies. If exact replay is inapplicable, `required=false` needs a specific mathematical reason, not a tool convenience or a claim that examples prove a theorem.

### Native projection repair

Read `/native_projection` in the review packet and its `field_directory`, counts, validation, files and existing hashes. The authoritative regime index is `/files/identity.json/regime_predicates`. `/files/machine_tags.json/registry` is an optional copy of `profile.registry`, not a second regime index. Inspect the actual frozen generated fields; a missing field invented by a reviewer is not a missing scientific entry. Use `artifact_get.value_digest` for a selected value; copy the prepared bundle/file hashes instead of reconstructing their serialization.

If the only publication defect is a generated sidecar protocol problem, report the actual issue with `kind=protocol_error`, only profile/publication blocks, and `projection_repairs=[{issue_id,file,pointer,reason}]` using a file-relative pointer. The controller regenerates sidecars and requests a fresh independent review once, including when the bundle is already valid and the field-location diagnosis needs correction. It does not ask the author to rewrite generated files or approve anything automatically. Read `projection_repair_ref` when present, independently resolve/carry every prior issue, and repeat all six joint checks and current scientific applicability checks. Scientific defects, erroneous labels arising from substantive tag facts, missing regimes in the scientific profile, and unsupported bounds stay on the ordinary research revision path. Never relabel them as mechanical issues to obtain this routing.

# Independent Profile and Bound Update Reviewer: Collaboration Protocol

Shared fields, references, tools, recovery, and writing rules are defined in the project-root `protocol.md`. This file defines only the meaning of this role's artifacts. The current schema is the sole machine-interface authority: call schema_read first rather than guessing default structures from documentation examples.

## Main artifact

kind=`review`.

review_type=profile; subject_ref identifies the controller-frozen proposal, including research_ref/profile_ref/prepared_ref/identity_ref. prepared_ref is the final five-file snapshot; verify that its label/identity sidecars agree with the profile. Record key bound/proof/bracket/profile assessments separately. approved_bound_ids lists only bounds admissible in the active bracket. For action=no_change, there is no proposal: subject_ref points directly to research, and review_type remains profile. Verify the basis for no change as well; approval closes the case without a write receipt. Unknown matters may be quarantined. Do not approve publication when an identity blocker or an unsupported active bound remains.

## Writing and handoff

When the controller supplies review_packet_ref, begin with its /overview and /review_scope, using artifact_get or read_batch. The frozen /evidence index groups versioned source locators, PDF offsets when explicitly located, and formal proof dependencies by result. Use source_read and artifact_get on the originals to inspect theorem hypotheses, proofs and counterexamples. The index contains no independent finding. Read the current prepared_ref at /files/profile.json/joint_analysis through its last page; a summary, a previous reading, or reading this index cannot satisfy this review.

In delta mode, /prior_issues gives every prior issue verbatim, and /differences indexes additions, removals and modifications across all five files. Before/after pointers are relative to the named old/new file. Follow them to the actual changes, resolve each previous blocker or carry it forward, and revisit everything affected, including cross-regime envelopes, conditions, evidence and tag projections. Use verified bound/proof candidates to avoid re-proving unchanged certified propositions. Missing or ambiguous scope calls for fresh review. Verify all six joint checks, current applications, five-file projections and every active bound before signing; always record newly discovered counterexamples.

First read the short proof_context_ref catalog. First-time proof_approvals require full independent verification, /common_specification and all necessary definitions. Set assumptions_complete=true only when these and the proof/dependencies fully express the assumptions. State every blocker's impact in proof_issue_scopes; unknown scope remains conservative. Leave credentials empty when unsupported and perform full scientific review. Use the focused-scope-review scope path only for actual controller-listed candidates; do not claim reuse without verified reuse credentials.

For snapshot-bound-scope revisions, read and bind delivery_scope_ref in coverage_audit. It pins the first reviewed delivery, original finite evidence and mandatory first-review issues. Finish those obligations and audit the actual changes; do not initiate another optimization of valid algorithm constants on every review. Correctness errors, false optimality, unsupported adoption and source/oracle/applicability defects may block at any round. A new coverage publication blocker must give its basis, valid affected_profile_pointers, and formal evidence; frozen_obligation additionally names actual frozen_issue_ids. A newly invented compatible stronger construction is not by itself a defect in an explicitly partial registered envelope.

Preserve such extensions as typed coverage_audit.followups: obligation_id, scope, description, reason_nonblocking, evidence_refs, source_review_ref and retained_claims_valid=true. This declaration requires scientific verification that all retained claims are valid; it cannot excuse an incorrect theorem or false best-known/optimality statement. The obligation binds the existing joint-parameter synthesis ledger and actual cells/comparisons. Established matching closures may use unpublished plus a pending closed_region_publication; other proved improvements retain their exact result/proof and affected regions as partial synthesis. Do not hide a known stronger result behind an unassessed remark. If the frozen profile has not recorded a new finding, return it for the author's local registration instead of signing a different future document. On the next review, check that registration and its consequences, without opening a further algorithm-design task. Approval requires identical persisted followups in research, profile and this audit, with all six joint checks still true.

For read-only inspection, prefer read_batch over serial independent artifact/source reads. draft_outline and draft_read_batch support only drafts already accessible under this role's existing permissions; they do not grant access to the author's mutable workspace. Inspect the frozen profile/proposal through artifact_get when no accessible draft exists.

In a subsequent review, verified unchanged independent-proof credentials in the catalog may reduce repeated reading of original proofs. Fill proof_reuse_context_ref and reused_proof_refs, and explicitly confirm them in the current proof_approvals. Still review the current bounds' applicability, new transfers/compositions, coverage, comparisons, reader, and prepared five files in full; list every current active bound in approved_bound_ids. New counterexamples, invalid dependencies, or undeclared assumptions may revoke reuse at any time. Even if the proposal as a whole receives revise, a correct independent proposition with complete scope may receive its own credentials; do not issue credentials for a proposition under a direct proof blocker.

For bound-review-context tasks, coverage_audit is required. First read the nine checks in coverage_scope_ref and review_context_ref, which contains short differences, prior review, and reuse candidates. Each check must state its actual scope, verified/not_applicable/unresolved, and specific reason; verified entries must bind result/proof artifacts. Address previous publication blockers individually through resolved_issue_ids or carried_issue_ids, retaining carried items in issues. For a new coverage publication blocker, give issue_id/category/why_new/affected_subjects in new_blocker_justifications, and attach formal evidence_refs to the issue. The first review must consolidate all mandatory obligations discovered. New scientific errors may always block publication.

When reusing prior per-bound credentials, coverage_audit.reuse_context_ref must bind the same input reference, and reused_bound_ids may select only verified candidates in context.reusable_bound_ids. Still verify current applicability/composition and explicitly include the bounds in approved_bound_ids. A candidate does not compel approval; new counterexamples or undeclared implicit dependencies invalidate it. Always review comparisons/summary/reader/five files.

Use target_schema_read to verify the target format. Preflight/prepare establishes mechanical handoff validity only. Independently check scoped-fact semantics, each view's permissions, complete conditions, regions, and approved bounds. Missing common axes may have null sources and unknown status, but actual view declarations must be preserved. Reject nonexistent source Pointers and unregistered navigation concepts labeled controlled. Do not substitute the producer's preflight report for your own review.

Save the body with artifact_put(kind,json_text,dependencies), or freeze a draft assembled in chunks. Dependencies reference saved formal artifacts only; do not inline their full texts. Use artifact_get(ref,pointer,offset,limit), source_read/source_search, atlas_read, and resource_read as needed, always respecting this invocation's tool permissions and pagination.

The final output structure is fixed: `status` (complete / needs_materials / blocked), `artifact_refs` (actual references), `summary` (a short English explanation), and `checkpoint_ref` (a fixed reference or null). complete means that this role's responsibility is finished; it does not automatically mean that research conclusions are approved or committed. State materials requests/blockers in formal issues or checkpoints, identifying the affected subject, scope, and next materials required.

Unknown does not automatically mean erroneous. Revise for explicit extraction omissions/scientific errors; preserve pending verification when materials are insufficient. Protocol repair must not remove a scientific rejection, and scientific revision must not overwrite historical versions. After handoff, the controller assigns the next responsibility and independent review.
