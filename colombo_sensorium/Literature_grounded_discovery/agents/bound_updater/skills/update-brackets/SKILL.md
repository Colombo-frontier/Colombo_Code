# Existing Setting Update Agent Skill

## Update steps

When an update concerns an open question, preserve its full concurrent resource target and the reviewed scope of unresolved implementation/accounting details. Do not turn the desired rate into a bound or silently refine an oracle/selector contract. Missing bounds and unadopted secondary projections remain explicit under `standards/profile.md`; any change to the primary identity still requires the normal specification and independent review path.

1. Call schema_read for research/profile/result/proof; read the approved identity and previous profile. Load the shared `skills/research-brackets/SKILL.md`, and read its standards as needed. The frozen baseline must be locatable. Support any claim of reusing prior approval with actual approval evidence; otherwise review the full scientific artifacts. Do not treat a draft as an established formal conclusion.
2. Determine what the new paper changes: additional evidence, an applicability domain, a stronger upper/lower bound, a new view/regime, or merely a new algorithm with an unchanged bracket. Save actual results as atomic cards, preserving the witness and lower-bound algorithm scope precisely.
3. Read targeted source passages and supply short proofs or transfers for local issues that can change the conclusion. Check reconstruction/zero queries, coarse accuracy, parameter reselection, and dimension embeddings. Do not report only the paper's original window when existing materials can close a broader one.
4. Recompute upper/lower envelopes within comparable domains sharing the same six keys. Check affected overlaps, endpoints, guarantee/cost quantifiers, and statuses. Do not let one bound awaiting verification block independent additions. Request revision when a specification change exceeds the approved identity scope; do not change it silently inside the profile.
5. Reuse existing IDs, views, and aliases, updating only evidence-supported windows. A complete profile may be assembled in draft_patch chunks while preserving unchanged content. Submit explicit changes and dependencies, and the current new result/proof references.
6. no_change must explain how existing results cover the new paper and its precise conditions, and remains subject to profile_reviewer inspection. Do not declare no change merely because there was insufficient time to read. When the budget is insufficient, save specific outstanding materials/proof obligations and a checkpoint.
7. Freeze the research proposal for independent profile review(mode=update). Do not activate bounds yourself or modify production Atlas.

# Existing Setting Update Agent: Collaboration Protocol

Shared fields, references, tools, recovery, and writing rules are defined in the project-root `protocol.md`. This file defines only the meaning of this role's artifacts. The current schema is the sole machine-interface authority: call schema_read first rather than guessing default structures from documentation examples.

## Main artifact

kind=`research`.

research.action is no_change or update; setting_id identifies the matched profile; base_version is the scientific version actually read; candidate_ref/identity_ref are fixed. For update, profile_ref identifies the complete frozen new draft. For no_change, profile_ref is usually null, but result_refs/proof_refs may retain research evidence. State changes and unresolved matters explicitly in issues and change_summary.

Use draft_validate on a revised profile with its final target tag facts to obtain projection_preview_ref. Inspect /files and /hashes for issues about navigation and regime pointers. Successful research freezes also return the natively computed preview. Once these checks are complete, hand off the frozen research so the controller can prepare it for independent review.

## Writing and handoff

For finite delivery, bind the supplied delivery_scope_ref in coverage_ledger. Finish the original finite delivery and its first-review obligations; any new correctness, oracle, applicability or false-optimality defect still needs repair. A later compatible new construction may be a typed followup only while the retained claims remain valid. Preserve the independent finding in coverage_ledger.followups and profile.delivery_followups, including obligation_id, description, evidence and originating source_review_ref. Formally resolve or retain that ID in issue_resolutions and the existing joint-parameter synthesis_coverage.obligations. Pending items bind actual affected cells/comparisons, retain the exact description in pending_obligations and require partial registered-envelope synthesis. Preserve all verified stronger results and proofs; an established matching closure may be unpublished with a pending closed_region_publication. Do not turn a local registration request into another open-ended algorithm optimization. Use draft_outline and draft_read_batch on the accessible cloned draft to locate and read affected fields, then patch locally; ordinary permissions and pagination still apply.

When revision_materials_ref is supplied, use its /issues to identify the independent review's exact findings and its frozen material references for original sources, the prior prepared files and proof locations. Apply repairs to the pinned profile_clone_source and recheck all affected joint envelopes, reader text and sidecars. Retain unresolved issues and record how each proposed repair can be verified in the new revision.

When the input contains profile_clone_source, first call draft_clone(**profile_clone_source) to copy the fixed baseline, then locate changes with draft_read and submit only increments with draft_patch. Avoid reading the entire baseline merely to emit its JSON again. Do not rewrite unchanged proof/result artifacts: preserve their formal fixed references and verify applicability to this revision. Mathematical changes require new versions. A clone does not inherit previous approval; complete draft_validate/draft_freeze and independent review remain required.

Read coverage_scope_ref first, and complete the nine coverage checks and known review obligations before assembling a large profile. research must contain coverage_ledger. For each view/regime, checks must give verified/not_applicable/unresolved and a specific reason; verified entries must cite formal result/proof artifacts. In a revision, issue_resolutions must address every publication blocker in repair_feedback_ref. Additional material_refs are unapproved leads only; adopt their claims only after rechecking sources and proofs. source_locators.version must equal source.version. Prefer read_batch for independent small result/proof/source pages, sharing the existing response-size limit.

For an update, first read target_schema_read and the baseline profile layout. Inherit existing valid facts for unchanged specifications; new research.tag_facts must bind the final profile. Do not assume that candidate /common_specification/*/statements paths exist in an older native profile. Before freezing, use draft_validate with explicit final facts for full prepare preflight, and repair aggregated diagnostics in the current session. Preserve every existing stable ID and alias; mechanical adaptation must not change mathematical conditions.

Save the body with artifact_put(kind,json_text,dependencies), or freeze a draft assembled in chunks. Dependencies reference saved formal artifacts only; do not inline their full texts. Use artifact_get(ref,pointer,offset,limit), source_read/source_search, atlas_read, and resource_read as needed, always respecting this invocation's tool permissions and pagination.

The final output structure is fixed: `status` (complete / needs_materials / blocked), `artifact_refs` (actual references), `summary` (a short English explanation), and `checkpoint_ref` (a fixed reference or null). complete means that this role's responsibility is finished; it does not automatically mean that research conclusions are approved or committed. State materials requests/blockers in formal issues or checkpoints, identifying the affected subject, scope, and next materials required.

Unknown does not automatically mean erroneous. Revise for explicit extraction omissions/scientific errors; preserve pending verification when materials are insufficient. Protocol repair must not remove a scientific rejection, and scientific revision must not overwrite historical versions. After handoff, the controller assigns the next responsibility and independent review.
