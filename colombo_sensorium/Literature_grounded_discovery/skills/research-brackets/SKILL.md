# Tool-Driven Bracket Research

Shared by bound_updater and profile_researcher. Independent reviewers apply the same scientific standards without reusing producer memory. The allowed tools and their schemas are those of the current invocation.

## Startup

Read the frozen candidate, approved identity, and relevant source catalog; for an update, also read the previous profile/base_version. Use schema_read for the complete result, proof, profile, and research structures. Retrieve these with resource_read as needed:

- `standards/evidence.md`: atomic results, fixed references, adoption, and quarantine.
- `standards/bracket_reasoning.md`: short proofs, transfers, six-key comparability, and envelopes.
- `standards/profile.md`: reader structure, statuses, English, and self-containment requirements.

Do not copy all historical papers, unread search leads, full-library exclusion archives, or large schemas into one request. Catalogs provide navigation, not substitutes for decisive source text.

## Research loop

1. **Select an obligation:** target an issue that can change the principal bracket, applicability domain, or missing-side status. State a verifiable completion condition for the next step, such as establishing randomized-algorithm quantifiers for a lower bound or covering a coarse-accuracy window using existing results.
2. **Obtain the source:** searches first produce leads; source_fetch fixes full text, source_search locates definitions/theorems, and source_read paginates necessary passages and premises. Prefer existing materials of the same version. Preserve supplementary versions/errata as new fixed references rather than overwriting original evidence.
3. **Save an atomic result:** give complete conditions, algorithm class/witness, guarantee/cost quantifiers, parameter domain, source locator, and six-key comparison contract. Repair invalid output under the protocol; register scientific uncertainties with their scope.
4. **Derive proactively:** first try inexpensive but potentially decisive baselines, monotonicity, parameter reselection, padding/embeddings, and envelopes. Save analyst transfers/derivations as self-contained proofs with accurate origin; do not attribute them to the source paper.
5. **Synthesize and check omissions:** construct a candidate bracket only from supported results or results awaiting review with explicit proposed proofs. Quarantine unproved claims separately. Check endpoints, degeneracies, and remaining windows, then search to address the most valuable gaps.
6. **Draft incrementally:** accumulate the profile with draft_create/patch, saving scientifically incremental content each time rather than repeatedly copying the full text. result/proof references are independent evidence; do not embed entire source materials in the profile.

## Pausing and completion

When a new context is needed, save a small checkpoint with confirmed result references, precise remaining obligations, source locations, and next steps. Do not copy existing formal content into memory. Preserve original archives continuously so that recovery does not depend on conversation history.

Record coverage and pending verification honestly when the budget is exhausted or materials are missing. Failure to find a bound proves neither nonexistence nor an open problem. On research completion, freeze the profile/proposal and submit it for independent review. Formal intake requires a writer receipt.
