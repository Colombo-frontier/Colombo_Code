# Joint-parameter synthesis standard, version 2

Apply to new research, existing-setting updates and independent profile review without changing the approved mathematical contract. Seeds, neighbors, drafts and old profiles grant no theorem or approval authority. Both producers and the existing reviewer receive this full standard and the same status contract. Exact fields follow `schema_read(joint_analysis)`, `schema_read(research)` and `schema_read(review)`; finish schema pages through `resource_read`. Do not embed full schemas in startup instructions or invent fields from prose.

## 1. Define the target before comparing rates

Fix the minimax quantity, algorithm/guarantee view, resource projection and full admissible domain. Distinguish these roles:
- `joint_variables`: variables optimized jointly in the primary question.
- `normalized_variables`: symbols, definitions and exact native `parameter_refs`; justify scaling before replacing scale/precision by dimensionless accuracy.
- `fixed_parameters`: theorem-fixed structural values, domains and native references where present.
- `uniform_in`: parameters excluded from hidden constants, including all joint variables.
- `allowed_constant_dependencies`: declared fixed values allowed in constants.

Prefer canonical `/parameters` IDs for joint_variables/uniform_in. Copy accepted display symbols or source parameter IDs exactly; never invent transliterations, abbreviations or aliases. Define a new normalized coordinate, its exact refs and scaling justification before use. Resolving references cannot change parameter identities or the quantity's scientific contract. Recording a variable is not jointly analyzing it: an O_c theorem for each fixed c need not be uniform in c. Do not hide dimension, accuracy, confidence, condition number or another joint variable in an undeclared constant. Precision-independent rates can be valid on proved zero-query/reconstruction-saturation regions; retain the full predicate rather than forcing every parameter into every formula.

## 2. Give regions their mathematical roles

Keep every region inside the fixed target domain; adding an endpoint cannot enlarge the contract. Distinguish source applicability, envelope crossover, saturation/endpoint, slice and unassessed complement. Applicability concerns a theorem plus its conversion. A crossover concerns dominance and may be asymptotic. Saturation/endpoint concerns triviality, exact reconstruction or another proved branch. A slice imposes a lower-dimensional relation. An unassessed complement lacks assembled/verified bounds.

A sufficient source dimension restriction is not an intrinsic phase transition without proof. Keep guards inside min/max envelopes; do not split unnecessarily at every source constant. `domain_map` records cells, native comparison IDs, states, `residual_domain` and `partition_argument`. `accounted` accounts for the entire target, possibly with explicitly unassessed cells; partial/unknown preserves the remainder. Disjointness/coverage require mathematical review, not string validation. Separate missing bounds, a quantified comparable gap, unassessed regions and invalid/inapplicable sources. Finite search cannot prove universal theorem absence.

## 3. Keep slices secondary

For epsilon=c*g(d) at fixed c, use `role: slice`, the exact `slice`, local fixed_parameters/constant_dependencies and c's allowed range. Keep the joint target primary. Substituting varying c into O_c does not make constants uniform: extending a slice needs explicit constant dependence or a uniform theorem. A ratio may cancel precision while both complexities retain it; display ratios separately from costs.

## 4. Recompute guarded envelopes

For each affected comparison, account for every mechanically compatible candidate in the profile and authorized catalog. Each `bound_uses` entry declares included/dominated/inapplicable/incomparable/unresolved, `validity_region`, `adopted_region`, reason and `mapping_proof_refs`. Seed selection is not proof of applicability.

Mechanical matching only locates candidates. Inclusion needs the right model, algorithm scope, guarantee, output, quantity, resource and guard. Do not merge quantity/resource IDs by labels; an equivalence transfer proves direction, cost and guard. Supply separate upper_envelope/lower_envelope/gap and justify any newly closed subregion. Report zero cost with an inapplicable ratio, never 0/0. Numerical probes expose errors but cannot certify uniform dominance or coverage. Excluded candidates cannot enter active native bracket-reference fields.

## 5. Separate provenance, validity and impact

For each new/changed adopted bound, record source_theorem/subclass_transfer/oracle_reduction/analyst_derivation, source/proof IDs, theorem/version locator, source domain, target validity domain and mapping. Schema-permitted retained_baseline means an explicitly fixed existing-profile dependency, not authority; recheck premises or use a current scoped credential. Seeds and external archive IDs never imply retained approval. Lower subclass transfers prove inclusion and preserve queries, outputs, guarantees and costs without imposing subclass assumptions on the target.

Each material finding's `impact` states old/new envelopes, validity/improvement/unchanged regions, witness, boundary type and argument. Distinguish bound improvement, zero-query endpoint, nontriviality_only, domain extension, correction, synthesis_only and no_effect. A one-call lower need not advance the main rate. `boundary_type: exact` requires constants and inequalities; distinguish asymptotic crossovers from exact activation thresholds. A valid bound can improve only one window and be dominated later; explain the windows. Equal status labels do not imply equal envelopes.

## 6. Formal research and exact independent review

New/updated profiles carry `joint_analysis.version: 2`; old analyses remain conditional history, never fresh completion/source certificates. Read the complete schema, preserve stable IDs/unaffected records and expose necessary normalization. Prose cannot repair contradictory native inequalities, merge identities or approve claims. Presentation-only findings cannot justify changed guards, envelopes, rows or statuses; mixed changes need scientific support for every affected comparison.

Research records `research_scope={queries_complete,unresolved_leads,scope}` for actual finite work, not exhaustive literature. Preserve exact lead strings in obligations; queries_complete cannot conceal interrupted search or unprocessed leads. Honest partial proposals may retain missing bounds/pending work if it does not undermine active claims; do not claim complete synthesis. Complete finite/no-change assessment needs complete v2 synthesis, finished declared search, resolved necessary normalization and no unresolved material lead. Known mathematical gaps may remain.

The existing profile_reviewer independently reads the frozen analysis and performs six `joint_review.checks`: parameter_roles, guarded_envelopes, domain_accounting, improvement_regions (impact), provenance, display_consistency. Each needs approve/revise/defer and a substantive reason. Bind prepared_hash=prepared.bundle_hash, analysis_hash to the current canonical analysis hash, and science_context_hash=prepared.science_hash. The science hash binds research context, baseline and contract fingerprints outside the five-file hash. Read/verify actual contents; preflight, hashes and neighbor approvals are not review. Any bundle, analysis, context or contract change invalidates approval. No third model role; Python checks structure/refs/hashes, not arbitrary inequalities.

Existing-setting no_change requires canonical baseline taxonomy, joint v2 with resolved target/complete synthesis, queries_complete=true and no unresolved lead. Research may retain profile_ref=null; the controller supplies science_prepared_ref, a read-only five-file snapshot with the same three review hashes. A baseline without v2 needs update or needs_materials, not fabricated no-change. No-change approval writes no Atlas files and issues no receipt.

## 7. Structured synthesis and native closure

New analyses use version 2. `synthesis_coverage` separates `gap_basis: registered_envelope` from `literature.status` (finite_scope_complete/partial/not_assessed) and its explicit finite scope. A recorded compatible-bound gap does not prove all literature or derivations assembled. Synthesis status is complete/partial/unassessed.

Within affected comparison_ids, structured obligations bind native bound/cell/proof refs, argument, state (resolved/pending/not_applicable) and affects_gap:
- compatible_candidates: selected/eligible candidates, overlap and guarded envelopes.
- replacement_branch: after withdrawing/excluding a selected claim, assess the remaining compatible branch, not just deletion.
- resource_projection: investigate other quantities/resources for directional transfer. Deriving a necessary sum-resource inequality from a component inequality needs proof and preserves distinct IDs. Component upper bounds from different algorithms cannot be added to claim one common algorithm.
- closed_region_publication: make a derived closure native or keep publication pending.
- material_lead: every exact pending_obligations/research_scope.unresolved_leads string is its lead, pending with its gap effect stated. Do not erase an unprocessed GAP-changing route by calling the envelope fully synthesized.

The controller lists bounded opportunities, not all mathematics; identify further affected projections, replacements and material routes. Explain a not_applicable contract/guard obstruction. Every pending obligation stays in pending_obligations; complete synthesis/no-change cannot contain pending obligations. Partial adoption may be valid when pending work does not undermine adopted claims, but grants no complete-research certificate.

Every domain cell has closure/publication/closure_proof_refs. Publication concerns matching upper/lower closure, not the existence of any comparison. `closure: established` requires a proved matching bracket. `publication: native_comparison` links nonempty native comparison_ids with status optimal and decisive native proof IDs. Established closure without a native comparison is unpublished, with a pending closed_region_publication obligation and partial synthesis. Prose/cells alone do not close native brackets. `closure: not_claimed` requires publication unassessed and empty closure_proof_refs even for cells linked to native comparisons; text must agree.

`impact.kind: domain_extension` asserts strict extension beyond a previously certified domain. Its extension_witness contains old_domain/new_domain, explicit outside_old_witness, membership/nonemptiness argument and native proof_refs. Independent review proves these and uniform constants. New regional/saturation registration without an outside-old witness uses explicit_region_registration and extension_witness:null; it asserts neither strict extension nor improvement. Other kinds also use null. No earlier Atlas record is not a domain-extension witness. Structure checks do not solve dominance, feasibility or nonemptiness.

Complete finite/no-change needs complete synthesis and finite literature scope, actual search/source-read obligations, and all six independent joint checks including publication and extension witnesses. Supported partial work is honest when marked. Neither means a solved setting or absence of stronger literature. Scientific closure publication is distinct from writer commitment; only a successful transaction receipt proves ingestion.
