Finite research planning, source recovery and operational coverage

Applicability

This standard applies when context.budgets.finite_research_scope is true. Otherwise retain the legacy tool and search-completion rules. It changes the operational research scope, not the scientific task, native comparison taxonomy, retained-premise contract, independent review or publication checks. No new scientific statement is approved by a research plan, metadata lookup, cache hit, candidate disposition or download recovery.

Plan before literature access

Read the fixed target and determine the relevant oracle, algorithm class, guarantee, output, quantity/resource identities, joint parameters, current guarded envelope and evidence dependencies. Before the first bibliography or source request, freeze a finite research plan with research_scope_freeze. Name the query routes, literal queries, providers, years, ranking/date semantics, cutoff, page/record limits and source obligations. Choose a bounded scope that fits the remaining cumulative setting and role budgets, with room for refinements, primary reads, validation, correction, checkpoints and final handoff. Limits are ceilings, not a requirement to spend every allowance.

Include at least one targeted task/oracle novelty route. Use known DOI or arXiv identifiers for bibliography_lookup instead of a broad title search when checking a known paper. A quoted title sent as a generic Crossref query is still fuzzy discovery. An exact identity lookup is metadata, not verification of a theorem. An unversioned arXiv lookup checks the currently exposed version and its dates; a version-specific lookup does not establish the entire revision history. First-submission search order alone does not cover revisions of old papers. Do not describe a provider as enforcing a date range that the request did not actually enforce. Check cutoff against exposed publication/revision events and the actual version of every decisive primary source.

The research_scope_freeze argument plan_json is a serialized JSON object. Its routes array is required. Each route supplies route_id, provider and the actual literal query; optional year defaults to null, limit to 10, max_pages to 1, max_records to limit*max_pages, purpose to novelty and refinement_of to null. Set the intended bounded limits explicitly. date_kind is submission for arXiv and publication for the other providers unless a supported alternative is explicitly declared; date_from defaults to the context window and date_to to its cutoff. A refinement route names its parent route and explains the extension.

Top-level source_obligations is an array. Each entry supplies obligation_id, kind, identifier and reason, with required defaulting to true, record_ids to an empty array and verification to primary_text. Use verification metadata_version for a known-source identity/version/correction check; it does not require a historical full-paper download or confer theorem evidence. Each actual source_fetch must name its predeclared obligation and purpose. For a closed_bracket_check plan also supply proof_check, all comparison_ids and exact baseline_refs, each with pointer and sha256. The controller checks complete observed frozen reads, not a model assertion. A self_contained_reason can explain a genuinely source-independent proof; it cannot waive an actual external premise.

Finite scope and immutable extension

The controller records the original plan. Additional routes or source obligations require a reasoned research_scope_extend; extensions cannot reduce old obligations or conceal already exposed relevant candidates. Do not shrink a plan after a difficult or potentially gap-changing result appears. A completed bounded prefix or explicitly finite date/ranking window is not an exhausted provider corpus. Preserve its exact unseen cursor, returned totals and reason as the unassessed complement. Provider pagination, new query directions, metadata exposure, exact lookups, source fetches/reads, stage tools and cumulative time have separate counters.

Read all records that belong to the declared finite prefix, using bibliography_read for omitted records in an already archived provider page. Such reads make no new HTTP request and do not spend a new-query allowance. Provider cursor pages retain their exact query identity and do not consume a new-query slot. Cache hits still count as tools and must be read in this role; they do not count as new network work or a scientific reading credit. Bounded retries still consume their actual request allowance.

Use candidate_dispositions to account for every exposed candidate. Explain an out-of-contract metadata screen against the fixed task; metadata alone cannot support a new rate. Plausible material candidates must remain pending until decisive applicability/evidence is checked. A checked scientific candidate needs actual own primary-source reads, exact source hashes and the normal finding evidence. Neither a page limit nor lack of time is a reason to mark a relevant candidate incomparable. A relevant unresolved candidate blocks complete no_change even if it is the last item of the finite prefix.

Source purpose and bounded recovery

Declare source obligations before fetching. Distinguish a primary-text premise needed for a conclusion from an optional historical citation/identity follow-up outside this finite check. Optionality must be justified in advance; a failed required source cannot be relabelled optional to complete a run. A source used to support a finding or changed proof is subject to the original decisive-read and independent-review gates. No unrelated paper can resolve another source's failure.

When enabled, source recovery may retry an oversized download under the configured file hard limit, cumulative byte/attempt budget and remaining deadline, or try a verified equivalent primary version/declared official location. Every attempt and limitation is retained. Successful download grants no source_read credit. Confirm actual identity, version and decisive text before using failure_resolve. Required evidence that remains inaccessible stays incomplete. An optional source follow-up must stay explicitly pending in the operational scope audit without becoming a scientific claim or a completed source verification.

Light maintenance for exactly closed registered targets

The controller's maintenance_lane is a screening hint, not a proof. A closed_bracket_check plan requires complete own reads of the frozen contract, parameters, views, quantities, resource projections, bounds, comparisons and proofs, exact baseline hashes, all registered comparison IDs and a proof_check explaining identical conditions, endpoint inclusion and the matching envelopes. An optimal label alone is insufficient. Review relevant source versions/corrections and perform the declared targeted novelty route. Reuse conditional accepted premises only within their recorded contract. Do not routinely download unrelated historical citations or expand into every adjacent oracle. New counterevidence or an unresolved material dependency reopens the necessary work.

Handoff and review

Keep outcome no_change, candidate_change or incomplete under the existing role schema. In scope.note bind the stable frozen plan_hash and state what was checked; record the unassessed complement and optional pending follow-ups accurately. Do not copy a changing whole-event-audit hash into its own handoff. scope.unresolved_leads identifies unresolved plausible changes/required work within the declared check, not a claim that unseen literature is absent. Preserve the complement in joint_analysis coverage as appropriate. Complete no_change still requires an actual successful finite novelty search, all existing comparison/joint checks and the original no-change evidence, including its primary-source own-read requirement when findings are nonempty. A supported local delta may remain committed_partial when wider work is pending. An incomplete handoff never advances a successful watermark.

The independent reviewer checks the scout's scoped evidence and every changed scientific dependency, using its own original reads. It does not adopt scout reading credit, candidate status or a prior approval. A reviewer may freeze a separate verification scope for its own bibliography/source work without pretending it is a new novelty census. Preserve exact prepared hashes, six joint_checks, canonical native status codes and the native five-file transaction. Operational reasons such as a source size limit or an unassessed tail are not new native complexity statuses.

When the reviewer context provides finite_verification_plan, its source-only plan is frozen by the controller with empty reading credit. Its required source_hash obligations refer only to granted immutable sources. No repeated novelty search is required for that verification role. Read the complete controller-pinned scout_research_scope artifact yourself and the decisive primary material yourself; extend the verification plan if additional identity/version research becomes necessary. The original scout witness remains unchanged and grants no verification approval.
