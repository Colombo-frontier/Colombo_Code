## Exact output conventions

Before deciding, independently check the prepared `joint_analysis` against the explicitly loaded joint-parameter standard and the actual native comparisons. If context `joint_analysis` has `provided_complete: false`, it is only a pointer/hash descriptor: use `artifact_read` with `name: prepared`, `pointer: /joint_analysis`, and follow `next_offset` until the required complete analysis is read. Do not set checks true from the descriptor alone. The six `joint_checks` keys mean:

1. `parameter_roles`: intended joint variables, normalization, fixed parameters and hidden constants agree. Fixed-parameter constants are allowed when declared; substituting a varying expression for a slice constant does not produce a uniform joint result.
2. `guarded_envelopes`: every compatible mechanical candidate is included or explained; adopted terms preserve validity/adoption guards and quantity/resource/view/guarantee compatibility or an explicit mapping proof. Recompute minima/maxima and resolve affected overlaps.
3. `domain_accounting`: cells and residual are relative to the actual target domain. Distinguish joint regions, slices and unassessed regions; justify complete partition assertions. Unassessed is not proved open, and source applicability conditions are not automatically phase transitions.
4. `improvement_regions`: compare old/new envelopes, not isolated formulas or labels. Verify validity versus actual improvement versus dominance, the changed parameter/logarithmic factor, and exact versus asymptotic thresholds. Endpoint and one-call additions are not main-rate closure.
5. `provenance`: each fresh/changed adopted bound has the correct source or retained-premise derivation, locator, source/target domains and mapping. Do not attribute an analyst transfer as a theorem directly stated by the source.
6. `display_consistency`: native brackets, bound-use ledger, formulas, guards, gap ratio, status and analysis agree. Treat zero-query branches separately, without fabricated positive ratios or `0/0`. No visual QA claim without actual rendering inspection.

These checks require mathematical judgment; schema validation and numerical sampling do not prove general inequalities or domain coverage. `synthesis_catalog` supplies candidates and exact pointers, not approval. `domain_map.status: accounted` may account for explicitly unassessed cells; it does not mean every cell was researched. Known gaps and unrelated unassessed regions can remain while a coherent local delta is approved. Unresolved premises or overlaps affecting that delta cannot.

Return only the exact reviewer schema object. Copy `setting_id`, `base_version`, `prepared_hash` and `joint_analysis_hash` from the controller context.
