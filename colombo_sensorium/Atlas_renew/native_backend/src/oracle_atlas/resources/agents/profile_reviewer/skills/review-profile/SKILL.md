# Independent Profile and Bound Update Reviewer Skill

## Review steps

After reading proof_context_ref, distinguish a reusable independent proposition from its application in this revision. A proof actually verified in full for the first time may receive proof_approvals with necessary context_pointers (at least /common_specification). Use proof_issue_scopes to enumerate the effects of every scientific blocker; an empty affected list must not conceal unknown scope. In a subsequent review, select only catalog-verified candidates, fill proof_reuse_context_ref/reused_proof_refs, and explicitly confirm them again in the current review. Review applicability/envelopes/coverage/five files anew. Recheck in full without a certificate, after dependency changes, or when source issues/new counterexamples arise. Do not omit assumptions or ignore blockers to increase reuse rates.

1. First call schema_read(review). Read research_ref, candidate/identity, profile_ref, prepared_ref, and mode from the frozen proposal; prepared is the final five-file snapshot. For action=no_change, the subject is research directly; verify its no-change justification as well. Load `standards/evidence.md`, `standards/bracket_reasoning.md`, and `standards/profile.md`; read the corresponding standards for new specifications/labels. Do not reread the entire annual bibliography queue.
2. Verify agreement between the specification and approved identity. Read differences and per-bound credential candidates in review_context_ref first. Formal assessments of unchanged proofs may be reused only after verifying role/subject/fixed snapshot/scientific environment and complete explicit dependency hashes, with no new counterexample. Recheck in full without credentials, after dependency changes, or if implicit dependencies are undeclared. The new review must still explicitly list every active bound and approve the current proposal. A first new-profile review is complete, not summary-only.
3. For every bound proposed for activation, verify the six comparison keys, actual upper witness/lower algorithm class, guarantee/cost quantifiers, full parameter domain, and source version. Check decisive steps in each analyst proof; a similar formula in a source paper is not a proof.
4. Pay particular attention to simulation costs/additional oracles, metric/probability conversions, fixed-object and worst-case quantifiers, randomized adaptive lower bounds, dimension lattices/padding, and coarse accuracy. An erroneous source lemma requires inspection of its dependencies and repairs; it does not automatically refute an independent main theorem.
5. Recheck envelopes and complete all nine coverage_audit checks. Consolidate every discovered executable mandatory closure obligation in the first review instead of adding only one separate corner each round. A request for stronger synthesis must give the exact domain, missing proof steps, and formal materials; explain why a new blocker was discovered in this round. A second review starts with previous resolutions and changes; new counterexamples or source/applicability defects may still block publication. Unassessed new directions may be explicit, nonexhaustive followups; a known short synthesis that makes the current gap/status false cannot be merely a followup.
6. Verify self-contained English, complete references/proofs, and, when reader_markdown exists, its point-by-point agreement with structured scientific fields. Verify agreement of the final prepared five files' eight-axis labels/parents, Exact scope, identity/aliases, and body; inspect statuses, gap_causes, and formula/table presentation. Use vocabulary_lookup for concept definitions and registered parent edges rather than guessing classes from keywords. Do not claim visual acceptance if rendering was not performed.
7. Fill approved_bound_ids per bound and quarantine unproved/inapplicable results. A document that honestly preserves a missing side may be approved. Unknown native identity, invalid versions, or an unsupported active bracket -> revise/defer. Historical issues may be partly resolved; an arbitrary unresolved item must not automatically reject the document.
8. Save the review. Review approval establishes adoption conditions only; the final writer still checks versions, identity, and transactions and issues an intake receipt.
