# Finite research and source recovery

## Declare a bounded check

Before using literature tools, the scout freezes a plan with actual query routes, provider semantics, date bounds, finite page/record prefixes and source obligations. The initial plan has a stable hash. Extensions are append-only, explain their reason and receive separate hashes. They cannot shrink away known work or erase an exposed plausible lead.

A completed finite check covers the declared prefix and material candidates within it. It does not claim to exhaust a provider corpus. A terminal provider response can complete a route early; a bounded prefix can also finish with an explicit unassessed tail. Unread downloaded metadata inside the declared prefix remains unfinished work.

The runtime-loaded research standard and `finite_scope.plan_schema()` define the exact plan shape. Supply real queries, not template placeholders. A route names a provider, literal query and stable route ID, with explicit page/record limits. A refinement identifies its parent route. Discovery date semantics differ from publication and revision checking; no universal server-side cutoff or global revision ordering is promised.

## Current configuration examples

| Control | Example allowance |
|---|---:|
| Distinct query routes | 6, including 2 reserved refinement slots |
| Network metadata-page and exact-lookup attempts | 24 per role |
| Provider pages per route | 4 |
| Exposed discovery metadata | 200 records per role |
| Ordinary novelty-prefix capacity | At least 25 records |
| Proof-checked light novelty capacity | At least 10 records |
| Successful source-fetch calls | 8 per role |
| Source-fetch tool attempts | 16 per role |
| Source-read calls | 32 per role |

These are bounded capacities, not quotas to consume or claims of adequate worldwide recall. A provider returning fewer or no records can finish its declared prefix early. Each tool invocation also consumes the role and cumulative setting allowance.

Provider pagination retains its query identity. A cached metadata page or exact lookup uses no new network-attempt allowance. `bibliography_read` exposes a saved metadata page without querying the provider. Cache reuse never grants scientific approval or primary-text reading credit.

## Track candidates and exact identities

Each exposed record receives a stable ID, a disposition and a reason. Metadata may establish a clear contract mismatch. A plausible mathematical lead remains pending until its necessary evidence is assessed. A checked-source disposition requires linked text actually read by the current role. Metadata screening cannot fabricate a theorem, dismiss an unknown material lead, or transfer an incompatible oracle bound.

Exact lookup resolves a DOI, an arXiv identity or a bounded public metadata URL without a broad title-search fallback. It records the returned source identity and available correction/update information. A requested older paper revision does not establish a complete current history. No reported relation is not proof that all corrections were searched. Newly exposed material alerts reopen assessment and cannot inherit reads from an earlier source.

A light closed-bracket plan remains a proof check. It requires actual complete frozen contract, parameter, view, quantity, resource, bound, comparison and proof reads, exact hashes, complete comparison coverage and a rationale for the matching envelope. An `optimal` status is only a hint. Targeted novelty and relevant correction checks remain necessary; a self-contained retained proof must explicitly justify why an optional historical source is unused.

## Required sources and recovery

Declare a source obligation and its purpose before fetching. Required primary evidence remains blocking until linked own reads and the existing scientific gates are satisfied. A predeclared optional historical source can be nonblocking only if it has no candidate dependency and is unused by fresh findings or changes. Its failure remains visible.

The examples allow an initial 20,000,000-byte file attempt, bounded recovery up to 80,000,000 bytes per file, a shared 160,000,000-byte setting-cycle allowance, at most 16 physical attempts and up to two identity-matching primary alternatives. Remaining deadline and tool limits still apply. A declared Content-Length refusal describes a response header, not a verified downloaded PDF size.

Successful archived body bytes are charged. Proven pre-body size refusal can charge zero body bytes; unknown failures are conservatively charged against their reservation. The append-only ledger is shared across roles and continuations for the same fixed cycle. An unsettled attempt blocks silent retry. Wire traffic is not fully observable.

Recovery must preserve the exact source identity and requested revision. A different paper cannot resolve a source failure. Fetch success or a verified cache hit is not a text read. Clearing an earlier source failure still requires actual linked reading and explicit resolution. Corrupted archives fail closed; automatic repair of every possible cache corruption is not promised.

## Limits that remain

The client uses configured fixed request spacing and cache/provider locks. It does not provide adaptive HTTP 429 cooldown or Retry-After-aware scheduling. Rate limiting may leave discovery, a provider page or a version check incomplete. Preserve that failure and the remaining work; do not declare the provider exhausted or silently discard its candidates. A later retry or continuation must be explicit and remain within the same cumulative accounting.

A sound local delta can proceed as partial work when its own evidence is complete, while wider discovery remains unfinished. Necessary failed evidence cannot support a new claim. A successful no-change result requires the declared finite check, all exposed material candidates, relevant alerts and required evidence to be completed. All source, baseline, prepared, taxonomy and independent-review gates remain in force.
