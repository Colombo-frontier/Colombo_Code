# Cell: Retrieval Around Proof Obligations

Five read-only operations are available through `scientific_memory`. Start with the current proof obligation; Upper may also consult Hard Families, and Lower may search for Algorithms that exploit a construction. Generic mode still permits only theorem records; do not attempt to exceed permissions by fabricating ref.kind.

Before actual use, read the complete statement, scope, source, and limitations, check assumptions through variable/parameter mappings, and verify Oracle/Metric/Guarantee/Resource conversions. Do not treat Unknown as satisfied; not requiring strong convexity does not prohibit strong convexity; do not combine conditions from different variants. Fresh samples cannot substitute for permission to make two queries on the same sample.

When adopting or seriously attempting to use material, place the fixed ref and content snapshot in the current research package and record direct/needs_transfer/inspiration_only/incompatible/insufficient_information. A lead cannot directly serve as an accepted theorem; it may be independently reproved and saved as a local argument. Continue truncated reads with next_offset. The Verifier receives only fixed material, without an added live reader.

Do not change the assignment, update LTM, invoke curation agents, or automatically turn usage records into new scientific knowledge. See `GUIDE.md` for details.
